# GROW WITH ME

## Product Requirements & Vision Document

### 1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW

**Product name:** Grow With Me

**Tagline:** Your Personal Growth Operating System.

**Platform:** Telegram Bot + Telegram Mini App.

**Initial audience:** One user — the owner of the system.

**Product type:** Personal development, habit tracking, journaling, knowledge management, analytics and AI-powered personal coaching.

**Core philosophy:**

Grow With Me is a personalized environment designed to help its owner become a better version of himself through consistent daily actions, self-reflection, learning, discipline and measurable progress.

The product must connect all aspects of personal development into one unified ecosystem.

It is not just a habit tracker, journal or productivity application. It is a personal operating system that helps the user understand his behavior, track his development, acquire knowledge and make better decisions.

The application must prioritize comfort, simplicity, personalization and actual usefulness over the number of features.

It should reduce friction, not create additional responsibilities.

### 2. PERSONAL IDENTITY

The system must be built around the user's desired identity.

The user is becoming a disciplined, knowledgeable, compassionate, spiritually developed, physically healthy and mentally strong person.

Core identity characteristics:

- Integrity: keeping promises, being honest and taking responsibility.
- Compassion: helping others, listening, respecting people and their boundaries.
- Spirituality: gratitude, humility, patience and continuous spiritual development.
- Intellectual curiosity: learning about psychology, philosophy, history, science, economics and technology.
- Discipline: consistency, time management and control over distractions.
- Physical health: regular exercise, adequate sleep and responsible nutrition.
- Emotional intelligence: understanding emotions, accepting criticism and managing reactions.
- Lifelong learning: constantly acquiring knowledge and developing professional skills.

The application must include a dedicated Identity & Principles section where the user can view and edit his identity statement, values and personal rules.

### 3. MAIN DEVELOPMENT AREAS

The entire application must be organized around five interconnected development areas.

**1. Spirituality & Character**

- Spiritual practices.
- Names of Allah and their meanings.
- Gratitude and humility.
- Moral principles and ethics.
- Good deeds and helping others.
- Personal character development.

**2. Intellectual Development**

- Psychology.
- Philosophy.
- World history.
- Science.
- Economics.
- Technology and artificial intelligence.
- Critical thinking.
- General knowledge and reading.

**3. Physical Development**

- Physical activity.
- Strength training.
- Walking and general activity.
- Sleep consistency.
- Recovery.
- Healthy lifestyle habits.

**4. Mental Development**

- Emotional regulation.
- Self-awareness.
- Patience.
- Resilience.
- Self-confidence.
- Reflection and personal analysis.
- Respecting personal and other people's boundaries.

**5. Academic & Career Development**

- University studies.
- Diploma project.
- IELTS and English.
- Software Engineering.
- Programming and architecture.
- Backend, frontend, AI/ML and DevOps.
- Professional development and projects.

These five areas must be visible in the Progress section and integrated into the habit tracker, knowledge system, reflection and AI analytics.

### 4. DAILY HABIT TRACKER

The application must contain ten configurable daily habits.

Initial habits:

1. Wake up at a consistent time.
2. Morning spiritual practice / reflection.
3. Read a book — minimum 10 pages.
4. Deep Work / studying — minimum 60 minutes.
5. English practice — minimum 20 minutes.
6. Physical activity — workout or walking.
7. Learn something new — minimum 20 minutes.
8. Do one good deed.
9. Evening journaling.
10. Sleep on time.

Each habit must have:

- Name.
- Description.
- Icon.
- Development category.
- Target value.
- Minimum, Target and Bonus completion levels where applicable.
- Reminder settings.
- Active/inactive status.
- Completion history.

Habit completion must be extremely simple, ideally one tap.

The user must be able to customize, reorder, edit and replace habits.

The system must support daily completion tracking, historical records, completion percentages, streaks and weekly/monthly statistics.

A missed habit must not reset the entire system or make the user feel like the day is wasted.

Principle: Never miss twice.

The application should encourage consistency rather than perfection.

### 5. TELEGRAM BOT & NOTIFICATIONS

The Telegram bot is the main notification and quick-interaction layer.

It must support configurable notifications for:

- Morning greeting and daily overview.
- Daily habits.
- Upcoming habit reminders.
- Daily knowledge topic.
- Name of Allah of the day.
- Evening reflection reminder.
- Sleep reminder.
- Weekly progress review.

All notification schedules must be customizable.

The user must be able to enable, disable and change the timing of individual notification types.

Notifications should be useful, concise and non-intrusive.

The bot should support inline keyboard actions where possible, allowing the user to complete habits, open learning materials or access reflection without navigating through unnecessary screens.

The Mini App is the primary interface for detailed interactions.

### 6. DAILY KNOWLEDGE

A dedicated Daily Knowledge module.

Every day the system selects an educational topic from the user's configured interests.

Possible categories:

- Psychology.
- Philosophy.
- History.
- Science.
- Biology.
- Physics.
- Mathematics.
- Economics.
- Finance.
- Technology.
- Artificial Intelligence.
- Computer Science.
- Communication.
- Critical Thinking.
- Culture and Civilization.
- General Knowledge.

The daily topic must contain:

1. Topic title.
2. Category.
3. Short introduction.
4. Detailed but accessible explanation.
5. Interesting facts or examples.
6. Practical applications.
7. One reflection question.
8. Optional additional reading resources.

The content should be educational and accurate, not superficial motivational text.

The user must be able to:

- Mark a topic as read.
- Save it to favorites.
- Add personal notes.
- Ask the AI to explain it further.
- Request examples.
- Ask follow-up questions.
- Review previously studied topics.

The system must maintain a personal knowledge history and avoid unnecessary repetition.

Topic selection should be randomized but balanced across the selected categories.

### 7. SPIRITUAL GROWTH — NAMES OF ALLAH

A dedicated spiritual learning module.

The system should provide a daily Name of Allah (Asma ul-Husna).

Each entry should include:

- Arabic name.
- Transliteration.
- Meaning in Russian.
- Explanation of its meaning.
- Reflection on how understanding this name relates to one's life and character.
- Reliable religious source or reference.

The content must be handled carefully and respectfully.

Meanings and religious explanations must be verified using trustworthy Islamic sources. The AI must not invent religious interpretations or present uncertain claims as established facts.

The user should be able to save favorite names, read previous entries and add personal reflections.

### 8. PROGRESS & ANALYTICS

A dedicated dashboard showing personal development over time.

The main dashboard must display:

- Daily completion percentage.
- Weekly completion percentage.
- Monthly overview.
- Habit completion statistics.
- Development area statistics.
- Consistency trends.
- Reading progress.
- Learning activity.
- Physical activity.
- Academic activity.

The five development areas must have separate progress indicators:

1. Spirituality & Character.
2. Intellectual Development.
3. Physical Development.
4. Mental Development.
5. Academic & Career Development.

The system must distinguish between objectively measurable activity and subjective self-assessment.

Objective metrics include:

- Completed habits.
- Reading pages.
- Study time.
- English practice time.
- Workout sessions.
- Walking/activity records.
- Number of studied topics.
- Reflection completion.

Subjective metrics include weekly self-assessment scores from 1 to 5 for each development area.

The system must never treat a subjective score as an objective measurement of personal worth.

Historical data must be retained and displayed through useful charts and trends.

The user should be able to compare different weeks and months.

### 9. DAILY REFLECTION & JOURNAL

An evening reflection module.

The user should receive a configurable evening reminder to complete a short daily reflection.

Initial questions:

1. What did I do well today?
2. What could I have done better?
3. What did I learn today?
4. Did I treat people well?
5. What am I grateful for?
6. What should I improve tomorrow?

The questions must be configurable.

The user should also have the option to write freely without answering structured questions.

Additional functionality:

- Save daily reflections.
- View previous entries.
- Search journal history.
- Add tags.
- Link entries to dates.
- Add tomorrow's tasks and intentions.
- View AI-generated summaries and observations.

AI analysis must be optional and user-controlled.

The AI can identify recurring themes, behavioral patterns and potential improvements, but must not make unsupported psychological diagnoses or assume that journal entries reveal the user's entire personality.

### 10. PERSONAL NOTES & KNOWLEDGE MANAGEMENT

A separate Notes section for capturing ideas and thoughts.

The user should be able to create:

- Quick notes.
- Ideas.

- Quotes.

- Personal observations.

Each note should support:

- Title.
- Content.
- Category.

AI functionality:

- Automatic categorization.

The Notes module should gradually become the user's personal knowledge base.

A dedicated section containing the user's personal principles.

Initial rules:

3. Be kind, not weak.
4. Stay humble.
5. Respect everyone.
6. Control your emotions.
7. Never stop learning.
8. Respect your body.
9. Choose long-term benefits over short-term pleasure.
10. Be useful to others.

12. No phone during Deep Work.
13. Avoid unnecessary scrolling.

15. Never miss twice.

### 12. WEEKLY SELF-ASSESSMENT

A weekly review module, preferably scheduled for Sunday.

The user independently evaluates each development area from 1 to 5.

Evaluation categories:

- Spirituality & Character.
- Intellectual Development.
- Physical Development.
- Mental Development.
- Academic & Career Development.

The weekly review should include:

- Five self-assessment scores.
- What went well.
- What went wrong.
- Main difficulties.
- Most important achievements.
- What the user learned.
- Priorities for the next week.

The system should automatically collect objective data from the previous seven days.

The AI should then prepare a weekly report containing:

1. Objective statistics.
2. User's subjective assessment.
3. Changes compared with the previous week.
4. Strongest development area.
5. Area requiring more attention.
6. Recurring behavioral patterns, if supported by sufficient data.
7. Practical recommendations.
8. Suggested priorities for the next week.

The report must clearly separate facts, user opinions and AI interpretations.

No invented metrics or unsupported conclusions.

### 13. AI PERSONAL COACH

AI is a central component of Grow With Me.

It should act as a personal learning assistant, reflection companion and development coach.

The AI should have access to authorized application data, including:

- Personal identity.
- Goals.
- Habits.
- Completion history.
- Development areas.
- Daily reflections, when permitted.
- Notes, when permitted.
- Weekly reports.
- Studied knowledge topics.
- Personal principles.

AI capabilities:

- Explain educational topics.
- Answer questions.
- Help with self-reflection.
- Summarize journal entries.
- Analyze habit consistency.
- Identify behavioral patterns.
- Prepare weekly reports.
- Suggest realistic improvements.
- Help prioritize upcoming tasks.
- Search personal notes and learning history.
- Help the user understand his progress.

The AI must be honest, constructive and realistic.

It must not provide empty motivational messages or automatically praise everything.

It should be able to say when the user is inconsistent, procrastinating or setting unrealistic expectations, but without shaming or insulting him.

AI must distinguish verified data from assumptions.

### 14. OPENAI INTEGRATION

The initial implementation should use the OpenAI API through a backend service.

The user's ChatGPT subscription and OpenAI API billing are separate systems.

The application should use an independently configured API key and model.

AI context should be assembled from the application's database rather than relying on a Telegram conversation history alone.

The system should support:

- Persistent user profile context.
- Structured prompts.
- Tool/function calling.
- Retrieval of relevant journal and note entries.
- Access to habit and progress statistics.
- AI-generated learning materials.
- AI-generated weekly analysis.

The architecture should allow changing the model without rewriting the entire application.

The user should be able to control which personal information the AI can access.

### 15. PERSONALIZATION & USER EXPERIENCE

Comfort is a fundamental product requirement.

The application must be highly customizable.

Settings should include:

- Notification preferences.
- Notification schedules.
- Habit customization.
- Daily learning categories.
- Language settings.
- Theme settings.
- AI behavior and communication style.
- Reflection questions.
- Privacy permissions.
- Data export and deletion.

The application must not force the user to interact with every module every day.

The user should be able to complete essential actions quickly.

No unnecessary gamification, competitions, leaderboards, monthly challenges, artificial pressure or excessive notifications.

No feature should exist merely to increase the number of screens.

### 18. DATA PRIVACY & SECURITY

The application contains personal journal entries, habits, goals and potentially sensitive reflections.

Security must be treated as a core requirement.

Requirements:

- Authenticate Telegram Mini App users through validated Telegram initData.
- Never trust client-provided Telegram user IDs without backend verification.
- Ensure all records are scoped to the authenticated user.
- Protect API keys and secrets on the backend.
- Use secure database access and server-side authorization.
- Do not expose journal entries or personal notes to unauthorized services.
- Make AI access to journal and notes configurable.
- Allow data export and permanent deletion.
- Do not log sensitive personal content unnecessarily.
- Protect scheduled notification jobs and user preferences.
- Provide clear control over what information is sent to AI services.

The initial product is private and single-user, but the architecture should not rely on insecure hardcoded ownership assumptions.

### 19. PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

The following principles must guide every implementation decision:

1. Comfort over complexity.
2. Consistency over intensity.
3. Real progress over artificial gamification.
4. Facts over assumptions.
5. Personalization over generic recommendations.
6. Useful notifications over constant reminders.
7. Privacy by design.
8. AI as an assistant, not an authority.
9. Simplicity over unnecessary features.
10. Long-term usefulness over short-term engagement.

### 20. FUTURE EXPANSION

The initial version is exclusively for personal use.

Potential future expansion into a product for other users may be considered after approximately 6–12 months of personal usage.

The initial architecture should be maintainable and extensible, but there is no need to implement multi-user subscriptions, public onboarding, billing or commercial features at the beginning.

Do not build for hypothetical future users at the expense of the current owner's comfort.

### FINAL PRODUCT VISION

Grow With Me should become a personal environment that the user genuinely wants to open every day.

It should help him maintain discipline, develop his spirituality, expand his knowledge, improve his physical health, understand his emotions, succeed academically and become a better person.

The system should not measure how valuable a person is.

It should help the user see whether his everyday actions align with the person he wants to become.

**Grow With Me — Become someone you are proud of.**
